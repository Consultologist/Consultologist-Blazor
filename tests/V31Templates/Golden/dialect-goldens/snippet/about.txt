A snippet is a template rendered where it is placed, in the placing template's scope, and may place another snippet; its text is the author's to the byte, its file's final newline included (chapter 5 §§ 5.3.2, 5.6.3). The inner snippet is decided after the outer one, which places it.

package/ is the upload, byte for byte; entry.txt names the entry composed (node.golden). Composing it over scope.json (the data, keys rooted as the run holds them), with each placement decided as placed.json says (a site it does not name is not placed), gives expected.txt byte for byte: Scriban 7.2.5, the invariant culture, strict variables.
